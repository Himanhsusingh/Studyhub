🧩 Board-exam puzzle corner

Short reasoning practice for a quick brain warm-up.

Puzzle: The HCF of two positive integers is 6 and their LCM is 180. If one number is 30, what is the other number?

Show the method

For two positive integers: first number × second number = HCF × LCM. So 30 × second number = 6 × 180 = 1080; the second number is 1080 ÷ 30 = 36.
